About us

Two apartments, two addresses, one quiet village by the sea.

Mare and Gumbek are two independent apartments in Petrčane, a quiet village on the Zadar riviera — each at its own address. Each has its own entrance, kitchen, air conditioning, free parking space and outdoor space: Mare a garden and a large covered terrace, Gumbek a balcony and two floors.

Mare is a ground-floor apartment with no stairs, a garden and a terrace where summer life really happens — a good choice for families with small children. Gumbek is spread over two floors, with the sleeping area upstairs and two bathrooms, so it suits couples, friends or a family that likes a little more room. No reception, no corridors — the whole apartment is yours.

Travelling with more than one family? You can book both — they are in the same village, and for a joint enquiry we send one offer. Both are equipped for a real holiday: dishwasher, hob, coffee machine, TV, fast Wi-Fi and a free parking space.

Location

Petrčane — twelve kilometres from Zadar

Petrčane is a quiet village on the Zadar riviera: a harbour with small boats, a pine wood, a handful of taverns and pebble beaches. Close enough for morning coffee in the old town of Zadar, far enough aside that in the evening you hear only the crickets.

Zadar is an excellent base for exploring — the Sea Organ and the Greeting to the Sun, the Roman forum and St Donatus — while Kornati, Krka and the Plitvice Lakes all work as day trips. To the north lie Nin with its sandy beach and salt pans, and the channel below Velebit.

  • Donje Petrčane and Punta Radman beaches — about a ten-minute walk
  • Petrčane harbour and seafront — a few minutes on foot, taverns by the sea
  • Zadar centre and the Sea Organ — 12 km, about a 20-minute drive
  • Zadar Airport — 23 km, about a 25-minute drive
  • Nin, its sandy beach and salt pans — about a 15-minute drive
  • Day trips — Kornati and Telašćica national parks, Krka, Plitvice Lakes
